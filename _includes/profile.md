## Counselling and support for difficult relationships and emotional patterns

<br>


You might be finding yourself stuck in the same conflicts again and again, struggling to say what you really mean, or feeling misunderstood in relationships that matter to you.

### I work with people who...
- feel ashamed, "too much", or not good enough
- find themselves reacting more strongly than they want to
- feeling caught between looking after themselves and caring for someone else
- long for greater closeness but don't know how to get there
- know intellectually what they "should" do but find it almost impossible to do in the moment
- feel caught in painful relationship patterns
- have the same difficult conversations again and again
- struggle to express what they need
- long for closeness but end up withdrawing, attacking or shutting down
- feel responsible for everyone else's feelings
- struggle to know what they actually want
- find themselves overwhelmed by conflict

I offer a space where we can slow things down, understand what is happening beneath the surface, and find new ways of relating — with yourself and with others.
