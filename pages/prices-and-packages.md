I offer individual counselling, relationship and conflict support, and workshops, training and facilitation. Details and fees are below.

If you're not sure which kind of support would fit your situation, you're welcome to get in touch. We can talk briefly about what you're looking for and work out what might be appropriate.

## Individual Counselling

**60 minutes — £50**  

For people who want space to explore emotional patterns, difficult relationships, self-worth, shame, boundaries, conflict, or feeling stuck.  

[Find out more about individual counselling >](/counselling/)

## Relationship & Conflict Support
**90 minutes — £70–£100**  
_You choose where to land within this range._  

For two people or small groups who want support with communication, recurring conflict, disconnection or a difficult conversation.

This might be partners, friends, family members, colleagues, or others in an important relationship. The aim is to create enough space for the people involved to be heard and to understand what is happening between them.

[Find out more about relationship and conflict support >]()

## Workshops, Training & Facilitation

**Fees vary**

I offer workshops and experiential training around communication, conflict, relationships and Nonviolent Communication for groups, organisations and communities.

I can also design sessions around a particular situation or learning need.

Fees vary depending on the group, format and preparation required.  
Get in touch to discuss your needs.

## Concessions

I offer a limited number of reduced-fee places for individual counselling.

**If cost would make counselling difficult for you, please ask.** We can discuss what might be possible.

## Your First step

I offer a free 20-minute introductory conversation before you decide whether to book a session.

This gives us a chance to briefly talk about what brings you here, answer any questions you have, and get a sense of whether working together might feel like a good fit.
